# Founder Contribution Ledger

This document is a non-invoice contribution record for MACSENSE. It is intended to make the founder's work visible, organized, and credible without presenting itself as a demand for payment.

## Purpose

MACSENSE is not a single-screen prototype. It represents a multi-app body of work across concept, product design, Android implementation, engine design, browser implementation, audio systems, AI co-production workflows, and launch infrastructure.

This ledger records the categories of work contributed so the project history can be understood by collaborators, partners, investors, reviewers, and future maintainers.

## Contribution statement

The founder contributed substantial unpaid labor and direct project investment across multiple product generations, including:

- MACSENSE early/original version
- Mine Site, described by the founder as the real first version
- Android MACSENSE application work
- MACSENSE web/PWA direction
- Engine concepts and product architecture
- Visual identity and user experience direction
- Production credits and external tooling costs
- Iterative testing, design review, and feature specification

The founder estimates hundreds of hours of work across coding, design, product direction, testing, iteration, and production setup.

## Work categories

### Product architecture

- Defined MACSENSE as a music creation system rather than a conventional DAW clone.
- Established the vertical studio concept.
- Defined Ari as a bounded co-producer that proposes changes rather than silently mutating artist work.
- Defined Genetic Sound, SoundGenome, sound families, resurrection, breeding, lineage, and evolution as core product language.
- Defined Flow Capture as a recording workflow aligned with how artists actually punch in.

### Engine and audio concepts

- Directed the creation and integration of audio engine concepts including:
  - sound analysis
  - SoundGenome traits
  - lineage and breeding behavior
  - resurrection workflows
  - loudness and mastering direction
  - vocal preset scanning
  - exported proof artifacts

### Android application work

- Contributed to the Android version as a major implementation generation.
- Advanced the product beyond wireframes into a real app direction.
- Built or directed substantial Android-specific workflows, UI decisions, and engine behaviors.

### Mine Site / first-version work

- Mine Site is recorded as the founder's real first version and an important ancestor of the current MACSENSE direction.
- This prior work should be considered part of the invention trail and product lineage.

### Design and visual direction

- Developed the cyber-organic, dark-gold, premium studio direction.
- Pushed the product toward a high-value, expensive-feeling presentation.
- Established expectations that visuals must correspond to real product mechanics.

### Production and tooling costs

- The founder personally spent money on production credits, tools, and infrastructure necessary to move the project forward.
- Specific receipts, dates, vendors, and amounts should be attached separately when available.
